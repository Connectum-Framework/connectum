fix: with `propagateHeaders` on, a `subject`, `name`, `type` or scope that contains characters above U+00FF (Cyrillic, CJK, an emoji) is now admitted instead of rejected with `Unauthenticated`. It is written to the `x-auth-*` header as `utf-8''` followed by its percent-encoded UTF-8 bytes (the form of an RFC 8187 `ext-value`) and decoded by `parseAuthHeaders` and by `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` when it reads the standard `x-auth-*` names. When at least one text was encoded, the writer also sets `x-auth-encoding: percent-utf8`, and readers decode `utf-8''` texts only when that header is present, so a literal `utf-8''admin` subject from an older service or another gateway stays that literal text. ASCII and Latin-1 identities are written as before, with no extra header, with two exceptions that are encoded as well. A text that starts with `utf-8''` is encoded so that reading is unambiguous. A text with a space or tab at either end is encoded because `Headers.set` removes such whitespace from every header value: a subject of U+0020 followed by `alice` used to reach the next hop as `alice`, and the two subjects were one identity. This covers `subject`, `name`, `type`, each scope and a gateway `defaultType`; roles and claims are JSON and start with a bracket or brace, so they are not affected. NUL, CR, LF, a lone surrogate and a scope with a space are still rejected on write. The readers hold a decoded text to the same rules as a raw one: a decoded text with NUL, CR or LF (a percent-encoded `%00`, `%0D`, `%0A`) is refused with `Unauthenticated`, and other C0 controls and DEL are removed from a subject, type or name as the writer removes them; a scope is read as granted. `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` applies the same rules to the standard `x-auth-*` names it maps, raw or decoded; a header mapped under a custom name is still taken as sent. A downstream service that reads `x-auth-subject` or `x-auth-name` without `@connectum/auth` sees the encoded form for such users and should decode it when `x-auth-encoding` is present. `createClientGatewayInterceptor` encodes a non-Latin subject the same way. `defaultType` may now contain characters above U+00FF.
