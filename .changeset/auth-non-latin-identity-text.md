fix: with `propagateHeaders` on, a `subject`, `name`, `type` or scope that contains characters above U+00FF (Cyrillic, CJK, an emoji) is now admitted instead of rejected with `Unauthenticated`. It is written to the `x-auth-*` header as `utf-8''` followed by its percent-encoded UTF-8 bytes (the form of an RFC 8187 `ext-value`) and decoded by `parseAuthHeaders` and by `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` when it reads the standard `x-auth-*` names. When at least one text was encoded, the writer also sets `x-auth-encoding: percent-utf8`, and readers decode `utf-8''` texts only when that header is present, so a literal `utf-8''admin` subject from an older service or another gateway stays that literal text. ASCII and Latin-1 identities are written exactly as before, with no extra header; a text that itself starts with `utf-8''` is encoded too, so reading is unambiguous. NUL, CR, LF, a lone surrogate and a scope with a space are still rejected. A downstream service that reads `x-auth-subject` or `x-auth-name` without `@connectum/auth` sees the encoded form for such users and should decode it when `x-auth-encoding` is present. `createClientGatewayInterceptor` encodes a non-Latin subject the same way. `defaultType` may now contain characters above U+00FF.
