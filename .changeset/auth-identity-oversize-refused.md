"@connectum/auth": minor

fix: with `propagateHeaders` on, an identity that does not fit its `x-auth-*` header is now rejected with `Unauthenticated` (`Authentication failed`, not cached) instead of being cut or left out: a `subject` over 512 characters, a `type` over 128, a `name` over 256, or roles, scopes or claims whose serialized value is over 8192 characters (after the `propagatedClaims` filter). Before, two subjects that shared their first 512 characters became one identity downstream, and a role, scope or claim list over the limit was silently omitted, so a deny rule or an `authorize` callback that looks at it stopped matching. The readers are fail-closed too: `parseAuthHeaders` throws `Unauthenticated` for a value over its limit, `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` rejects a claims header over 8192 characters instead of reading `{}`, and `createClientGatewayInterceptor` throws `InvalidArgument` for a subject over 512 or roles over 8192 characters instead of cutting or omitting them. `setAuthHeaders` throws a `RangeError` (`HeaderValueTooLargeError`) and sets no header. A service that propagates identities near these limits must shorten them or turn propagation off.
