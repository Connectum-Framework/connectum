"@connectum/auth": minor

fix: with `propagateHeaders` on, an identity that does not fit its `x-auth-*` header is now rejected with `Unauthenticated` (`Authentication failed`, not cached) instead of being cut or left out: a `subject` over 512 characters, a `type` over 128, a `name` over 256, or roles, scopes or claims whose serialized value is over 8192 characters (after the `propagatedClaims` filter). Before, two subjects that shared their first 512 characters became one identity downstream, and a role, scope or claim list over the limit was silently omitted, so a deny rule or an `authorize` callback that looks at it stopped matching. The readers are fail-closed too: `parseAuthHeaders` throws `Unauthenticated` for a value over its limit, `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` rejects a claims header over 8192 characters instead of reading `{}`, and, for the standard `x-auth-*` names it maps, a subject over 512 characters, a type over 128, a name over 256 or a roles or scopes header over 8192 characters, whether or not `propagateHeaders` is on (a header mapped under a custom name is still taken as sent, and refused only when the identity is propagated), and `createClientGatewayInterceptor` throws at creation for a subject over 512 characters or roles that do not fit the roles header (measured as the writer measures them, with every character above U+00FF expanded to six), and `InvalidArgument` per call if the roles array was changed afterwards, instead of cutting or omitting them. `setAuthHeaders` throws a `RangeError` (`HeaderValueTooLargeError`) and sets no header. The limit is measured on the text as it arrives, before the writer or a reader removes C0 controls and DEL, so a value one character over the limit whose extra character is a control is refused, not accepted shortened. A service that propagates identities near these limits must shorten them or turn propagation off.
