fix: an identity whose `subject` is empty after the control characters that the identity headers remove are removed (for example a single U+0001) is no longer accepted on any path. `createAuthInterceptor`, `createJwtAuthInterceptor`, `createSessionAuthInterceptor` and `createInternalAuthInterceptor` end the call with `Unauthenticated` (`Authentication failed`, `JWT missing subject claim`, `Session mapping failed`, `Untrusted internal request`) and do not run the handler, with or without `propagateHeaders`; `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` does the same for a standard `x-auth-*` subject and for a subject under a custom `headerMapping` name (`Missing subject header from gateway`). A subject with other characters is passed on as it is (`a\x01b` stays `a\x01b`), and a subject of spaces or tabs is usable, because those characters are not removed. The same rule now decides a gateway type header: one that is empty, or empty after the control characters are removed, is treated as an absent header, so `defaultType` applies, for standard and custom type names alike. Options that fix the subject or type of every identity refuse such a value when the interceptor is created, naming the option: `sharedSecretTrust` `subject` and `type`, `meshIdentityTrust` allowlist `principal` and `type`, and `signedTokenTrust` `type` plus an issuer key that becomes the subject (no `claimsMapping.subject`); `createClientGatewayInterceptor` with a control-only `subject` and `createGatewayAuthInterceptor` with `propagateHeaders` and a control-only `defaultType` already failed at creation through the header writer. Such a subject used to be written as an empty header and read back as an identity without one, which was never valid.
